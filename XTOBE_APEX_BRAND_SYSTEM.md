# XTOBE APEX 3.0 — Brand system

## Brand identity

XTOBE is positioned as a premium deep-tech engineering company with a scientific, operational, and human-centered identity. The visual system favors precision, restraint, and clarity over spectacle.

Primary intent:
- Aerospace precision
- Swiss editorial typography
- Scientific instrumentation
- Premium industrial design
- Human safety and accountability

## Core proposition

XTOBE / APEX SYSTEMS

Supporting statement:
- INTELLIGENCE BEYOND THE INTERFACE.
- ENGINEERING INTELLIGENCE THAT PROTECTS LIVES.

## Visual palette

- Obsidian navy: #050B18
- Deep titanium: #142238
- Optical cyan: #67E8F9
- Ice silver: #DCE9F2
- Muted slate: #8196AF

## Design language

1. Structural clarity
   - Generous spacing
   - Strong information hierarchy
   - Clean editorial rhythm

2. Geometric precision
   - Modular grid and disciplined alignment
   - Angular, engineering-led forms
   - Subtle orbital and reactor-inspired accents

3. Controlled light and contrast
   - Cyan highlights used sparingly
   - High legibility on dark surfaces
   - Strong tonal separation without visual noise

4. Scientific credibility
   - Architecture diagrams should communicate systems, flows, and boundaries clearly
   - No decorative counters, fake telemetry, or unsupported claims

## Graphic principles

- The XTOBE monogram should feel engineered rather than ornamental.
- Reactor visuals should suggest sensing, processing, and human control.
- Use a premium but minimal palette with intentional cyan accents.
- Maintain readability in GitHub dark mode and light mode.

## Recommended use

- README hero: use a single high-contrast SVG hero with minimal copy.
- Architecture diagrams: use simple, labeled system flows with clear data boundaries.
- Project storytelling: lead with a flagship program and then supporting capabilities.
- Documentation: keep language technical, precise, and evidence-aware.

## Non-goals

- No imitation of another developer profile style
- No generic badge-heavy dashboards
- No fabricated metrics or promotional language without evidence
- No over-animated or noisy interface treatment
